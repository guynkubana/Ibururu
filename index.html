Terms of sale

These terms apply to every order placed on this website. By placing an order you agree to them.

Who we are

IBURURU is a clothing brand based in Kigali, Rwanda. Registered as . Add your registered business name and tax number in POLICY.legalName.

Prices

All prices are in Rwandan francs (rwf). Delivery is added at checkout, and the total you see before you pay is the total you pay. Say here whether prices include VAT.

Orders

Placing an order is an offer to buy. It becomes a confirmed order when we have received your payment and sent you a confirmation by email. Our pieces are made in small quantities, so if an item turns out to be unavailable after you have paid, we will tell you and refund you in full. If we cannot fulfil an order for another reason, such as a pricing error, we may cancel it and refund you in full.

Payment

You pay online at checkout, by mobile money or by card. Payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not see or store your card number or your mobile money PIN. If a payment fails, the order is not placed.

Colors and sizing

We do our best to show colors accurately, but screens differ, so a piece can look slightly different in person. Each piece lists its sizes and fit.

Cancelling an order

Write to us as soon as you can. If your order has not been sent yet, we will cancel it and refund you. Once it has been sent, our returns policy applies.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Rwanda.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version, and the version in place when you ordered applies to your order.

Shipping

We deliver anywhere in Rwanda for a flat rate of . The fee is added at checkout. We do not ship outside Rwanda yet.

Delivery usually takes after your payment is confirmed. We will message you when your order is on its way.

Delivery details

Please give a full delivery address (your district, your sector and a street, house number or landmark) and a phone number that works. The delivery person will call you on arrival. If we cannot reach you, we will contact you to arrange another delivery, and an extra delivery fee may apply.

Late or missing orders

If your order has not arrived within the delivery time, write to us with your order number and we will find out where it is.

Damaged on arrival

Check your parcel when it arrives. If something is damaged, take a photo and contact us within days. See Returns below.

Returns and exchanges

We want you to love what you ordered. If it is not right, you can return it within days of delivery.

What can be returned

Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition, with any labels still attached. List anything that cannot be returned, such as final-sale pieces or prints.

How to return an item

  1. Write to us by email or on WhatsApp with your order number and the item you are returning.
  2. We will reply with the address to send it to.
  3. Send the item back. You pay for the return delivery, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one. Confirm who pays for return delivery.

Exchanges

If a size does not fit, we will exchange it for another size when it is in stock. Because our pieces are made in limited quantities, we cannot always promise a replacement. If we cannot exchange it, we will refund you.

Refunds

Once we have received and checked your return, we will refund the price of the item to the payment method you used (mobile money or card) within days. Delivery fees are refunded only if the item was faulty or the wrong one.

Faulty or wrong items

If you receive a faulty or incorrect piece, contact us within days of delivery, with photos. We will replace it or refund you, and we pay for the return delivery.

Privacy

We respect your privacy. We handle your personal data in accordance with Rwanda’s Law No. 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

Check with a lawyer whether you must register with the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) as a data controller, and where your sign-up and order data is stored. The law restricts storing personal data outside Rwanda without NCSA authorisation, which matters for form, email and hosting services.

What we collect

  • When you join the list: your email address.
  • When you order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address and notes, the items you ordered, your payment method, and the mobile money number if you use one.
  • In your browser: your cart, the details you type at checkout and your light or dark setting are saved on your own device so you do not have to type them again. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Update this if you add analytics.

Why we use it

  • To take payment, prepare and deliver your order, and contact you about it.
  • To send you news of new drops, only if you joined the list or ticked the box at checkout. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the records we are required to keep, and to protect the site from fraud and misuse.

Who we share it with

Only the people who help us run your order: our payment provider (to process your payment), our delivery partners (your name, phone number and address), and our website and email services. We never sell your data. Name your payment provider, delivery partner and services, and say where they store data.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to run the business and to meet legal and tax duties. Say how long, for example 5 years. Email-list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it for marketing. Write to us at and we will reply. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to Rwanda’s National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA).

Children

Our website is not meant for children under 16. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

Security

We use secure payment providers and keep your details only where they are needed. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Contact

Questions about an order, a return or your data? Write to us.
